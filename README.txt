--- Quake 2: Stealth Edition ---

A mod that turns the action-packed Quake 2 into an immersive stealth experience. To download this mod, all you need is the .dll and .cfg files located in the midterm branch (and, of course, Quake 2 vanilla). Place these files in a folder with a shortcut to the game. You can name the folder whatever you like, but make sure to change the target of the shortcut executable to match the folder name. (right-click -> properties -> target). A copy of the source code is included as well. 

New features include:

1. Superpowers:

Get access to new "superpowers" that offer unique ways of avoiding/dispatching enemies. Dash past enemies with superspeed ('Y'). Avoid detection by turning invisible ('U') (only works IF enemies do not see you first, else they will jam invisibility!) Teleport directly to enemies to quickly dispatch them ('I'). Turn on invincibility to eat bullets with no consequences ('O'). Drain enemies of their health to heal you... making them weak ('P'). You can only use one power at a time and you must wait ten seconds before using another power, so use them wisely!

2. Weapons:

Revamped weapons include a pistol, sawed-off shotgun, a half-shotgun half-assault rifle hybrid gun, a burst assault rifle, an automatic grenade launcher (very messy), a c4 launcher that can be detonated ('l') after launch, a rocket that launches the player into the air, a plasma gun that can change the orientation of its shots ('K'), a magnet gun that pulls enemies towards you, and a modded BFG that moves all enemies away for a quick getaway. These weapons can be accessed using the digits 0-9, just like in the original quake. Go nuts.

3. Stealth Mechanics:

Metal Gear Solid's most iconic feature is arguably the alert system: normal, evasion, and alert. This mod has a version of this alert system designed to tell the player how much danger they are in. Lightning is also more important than before; enemies have a harder time seeing in the dark -- use this to your advantage! Crouching is another good way to go by unnoticed. Enemies will not hear you from behind if you are crouching. You could go guns blazing, but shooting bad guys unnoticed does significantly more damage with your pistol. Finally, determine if an enemy is nearby by crouching and sitting still. If the enemy is close by, you will know.

4. Level Objectives:

There are several goals you must finish to complete your subterfuge. For example, you will not begin the mission with powers or a full set of weapons. All supplies are procured on the field, so you need to obtain collectibles to obtain your equipment! Finally, once you get your supplies (or not), take enemies out from behind the lines to help your fellow soldiers!

Happy hunting!
